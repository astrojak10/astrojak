**Scope:** Public site at <https://www.astrojak.in/>

## Executive summary

ASTROJAK has clear purchase intent and a defined set of online Vedic astrology services, with published prices and a direct booking path. The current public site has three canonical pages: the booking homepage, service information, and plan selection. This supports conversion-focused searches, but leaves informational queries about Kundali, marriage compatibility, and consultation preparation largely uncovered.

The query themes below are **seed hypotheses derived from the site's services and FAQs**, not verified search queries. Google Search Console, keyword-volume data, live SERP positions, and difficulty metrics were unavailable during this check. Do not treat these phrases as evidence of demand or current ranking.

## Audience and intent hypotheses

| Audience hypothesis | Need or concern | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Person seeking personal or career guidance | Wants to understand what a Kundali consultation covers and how to book | Informational → commercial |
| Person considering marriage compatibility | Wants Kundli Milan, Guna Milan, or Manglik analysis | Commercial |
| Prospective online consultation customer | Wants price, appointment, payment, privacy, and refund details before booking | Transactional |
| Hindi- or English-speaking customer | Wants an accessible explanation and a consultation in a familiar language | Informational → transactional |

These are inferred from the site's published service descriptions, pricing, FAQs, and booking flow. Validate them against actual customer language and query data.

## Evidence and gaps

- The live site exposes canonical `/`, `/home`, and `/plan` pages; the sitemap is discoverable.
- The homepage presents booking and pricing; `/home` provides service information and FAQs; `/plan` supports plan selection.
- Existing site facts include service descriptions, INR pricing, appointment booking, payment, privacy, and refund information.
- Search terms, impressions, clicks, conversion rates, competitor pages, and ranking positions were not available. No search-volume or difficulty estimates are claimed here.
- The published expertise, consultation-count, rating, and outcome statements should remain accurate, substantiated, and consistent wherever they appear.

## Recommended next actions

1. Connect Google Search Console and export query/page data for the last 90 days; separate branded from non-branded queries and segment by country, device, and language.
2. Validate the candidate clusters with actual GSC queries and a keyword source for India, including Hindi/transliterated queries. Map each validated query group to one canonical page.
3. Prioritize a dedicated, useful matchmaking page only if it can explain the real process, deliverables, and limits without duplicating the booking page.
4. Build a small number of expert-reviewed guides from validated questions. Each should have a named author, transparent sources where factual claims need them, a clear update policy, and a relevant service link.
5. Measure qualified booking starts, completed bookings, and customer-support questions alongside impressions and clicks; review monthly.

## Claims requiring verification

- Search volume, ranking difficulty, and current positions for every candidate phrase.
- Which languages customers actually use for queries and consultations.
- Credentials, experience, consultation totals, ratings, and testimonial permissions before reusing them in new pages.
- Whether a new matchmaking or educational page would add distinct value beyond the existing service and FAQ content.
