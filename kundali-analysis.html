What is a Kundali reading?

What does a Kundali reading cover?

Houses and planets

The reading looks at the houses and planets shown in your birth chart and explains them in plain language as part of your personal Kundli reading. You can bring a specific question so the discussion is grounded in the areas you want to understand.

How the birth details shape a reading

In Vedic astrology, a Janam Kundli is interpreted from the planetary positions calculated for a person's birth time and place. The Lagna (ascendant), Moon sign, houses and graha placements are considered together; one sign or placement on its own is not the whole chart.
