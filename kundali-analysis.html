What is a Kundali reading?

A Kundali reading is a personal birth chart consultation with Acharya Narender Ji over a 1:1 audio call. Choose the standard reading for your birth chart or the detailed plan for Dasha, timing guidance and personal upay; a written summary follows on WhatsApp.

What does a Kundali reading cover?

Houses and planets

The reading looks at the houses and planets shown in your birth chart and explains them in plain language as part of your personal Kundli reading.

Dasha and timing

The Detailed Kundali Analysis includes Dasha and timing guidance to discuss periods shown in the chart.

Personal upay

The detailed plan also includes personal upay (remedies).
