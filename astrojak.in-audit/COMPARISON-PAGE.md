# Competitor Pages: Research and Opportunity Brief

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-31  
**Site:** https://www.astrojak.in/  
**Scope:** Public-page review and comparison-page opportunity research. Search rankings, keyword volume, and competitor performance were not available; this is not a SERP ranking study.

## Executive summary

ASTROJAK is positioned around a scheduled consultation with a named astrologer, published fixed-price plans, and a written summary on WhatsApp. The competitors reviewed show broader platform models: astrologer discovery/panels, instant consultation, and free astrology software alongside paid services.

There may be a useful comparison-page opportunity, but **do not publish a “best astrologer” ranking or unsubstantiated superiority claim**. Search demand for “[brand] vs ASTROJAK” was not measured, and the compared services are not identical. First validate demand with Search Console query data and confirm all details against the competitor pages immediately before publication.

## ASTROJAK facts observed

On the public [booking page](https://www.astrojak.in/) and [plan page](https://www.astrojak.in/plan), ASTROJAK describes:

- Sessions with Acharya Narender Ji, selected by appointment.
- Listed plans at ₹999 and ₹1,499; an introductory reservation flow is also advertised.
- A call-based consultation and a written summary on WhatsApp for applicable plans.
- Birth details submitted after choosing a plan and paying.
- Published privacy/refund information and a statement that astrology does not guarantee a result.

Recheck prices, inclusions, and booking flow before publishing a comparison because these can change.

## Comparison-page opportunity

### Recommended first candidate

**Working title:** “ASTROJAK vs AstroTalk: Scheduled One-to-One Consultation or Astrologer Marketplace?”

**Primary intent hypothesis:** A visitor comparing how to book an online astrology consultation and whether to choose a named astrologer or browse a larger provider panel.

This is a hypothesis only. No live SERP, query-volume, or difficulty data was available to validate whether people search for this comparison.

## Suggested page outline (1,000–1,500 words after evidence collection)

1. **Title/H1:** Name both providers and the decision being compared; avoid “best” unless objective criteria and evidence support it.
2. **Disclosure and methodology:** State that ASTROJAK publishes the page and explain the comparison criteria, sources, and review date.
3. **Short answer:** Explain who may prefer a scheduled session with a named astrologer versus browsing a broader provider panel; avoid declaring one universally better.
4. **How each service works:** Current booking steps, supported formats, and what happens after payment.
5. **Current plan and price comparison:** Cite live official pricing; clearly mark variable, unavailable, or unverified prices instead of estimating.
6. **Deliverables and continuity:** Compare reports, summaries, session length, follow-up, and astrologer choice only when the facts are confirmed.
7. **Privacy, refunds, and limitations:** Link to each provider's own current policy and explain that astrology does not guarantee outcomes.
8. **Who each option may suit:** Use transparent criteria (appointment preference, desire to browse astrologers, need for free tools), not unsupported quality rankings.
9. **FAQ:** Answer genuine decision questions in the visible page content. Do not promise FAQ rich results.
10. **Sources and last reviewed date:** Link to official first-party sources and set a quarterly review reminder.

## Recommendation and next steps

1. Start with a useful standalone service page for Kundali analysis or marriage matchmaking if it can offer distinct information beyond the existing booking page. This is likely a clearer fit than publishing several brand-vs-brand pages across a site with a small canonical page set.
2. Check Search Console for branded comparison queries and broader consultation queries over a representative period. Current connected GSC data has low volume; it cannot yet validate a comparison-page investment.
3. If comparison demand is supported, start with one transparently disclosed comparison. Do not create near-identical pages for several competitors without unique, sourced analysis.
4. Add author/reviewer attribution, a dated methodology, primary-source links, and a quarterly review date.
5. Measure qualified visits and completed bookings from the comparison page, not impressions alone.

## Limitations

- Search-engine result pages could not be reliably retrieved in this review; no ranking positions or SERP features are claimed.
- Search volume, difficulty, backlinks, and competitors' traffic were not measured.
- Competitor sites may vary content by location, device, account, or selected astrologer.
- Public claims on provider websites are not independent proof of accuracy, expertise, reviews, or service outcomes.
