After selecting a plan, choose an appointment slot and complete payment. Add the available birth details on the next page and note the questions you would like to discuss.

  • For one-person chart readings: date, time and place of birth.
  • For matchmaking: the same details for both partners.
  • If a time is unknown or approximate, mention that rather than guessing.
  • Choose a few topics or questions to help guide the conversation.

Relevant plans include a written WhatsApp summary or report after the call; see the plan descriptions for the specific deliverable.
