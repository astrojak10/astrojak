What is a Kundli matching consultation?

It is a 1:1 audio consultation with Acharya Narender Ji to compare two birth charts for marriage matchmaking. It is for couples who want a personal reading of both Kundlis, with a written summary on WhatsApp afterwards.

What is analyzed?

Guna Milan

Guna Milan follows the traditional 36-point Ashtakoot system. It compares eight factors, called kootas, to create a combined score out of 36; that score is one part of the reading, not a verdict on a relationship. Acharya Ji can explain how the traditional factors are considered in the context of both charts.

Varna Vashya Tara Yoni Graha Maitri Gana Bhakoot Nadi

Manglik analysis

Both Kundlis are checked for Manglik factors as part of the matchmaking analysis, with the findings discussed as one part of the wider consultation.

Dasha compatibility and timing

The consultation also considers Dasha and timing guidance, and includes personal upay (remedies). You can ask how the chart interpretations relate to the questions you and your partner want to explore.

Guna Milan is one traditional comparison, not a substitute for understanding both charts and talking together. Read more about Dasha and Mahadasha or prepare for a personal Kundali analysis.
