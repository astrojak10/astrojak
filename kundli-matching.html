What is a Kundli matching consultation?

It is a 1:1 audio consultation with Acharya Narender Ji to compare two birth charts for marriage matchmaking. It is for couples who want a personal reading of both Kundlis, with a written summary on WhatsApp afterwards.

What is analyzed?

Guna Milan

Guna Milan follows the traditional 36-point Ashtakoot system. It compares eight factors, called kootas, to create a combined score out of 36; that score is one part of the reading, not a verdict on a relationship.

Manglik analysis

Both Kundlis are checked for Manglik factors as part of the matchmaking analysis.

Dasha compatibility and timing

The consultation also considers Dasha and timing guidance, and includes personal upay (remedies).

What you receive

  • A 1:1 audio call with Acharya Narender Ji and a personal reading of both Kundlis.
  • Guna Milan, Manglik analysis, Dasha and timing guidance, and personal upay.
  • A written summary on WhatsApp after the call.

What details should you share?

Share the date, time and place of birth for both partners. If either birth time is unknown, share what you know; Acharya Narender Ji will explain what guidance is possible with the details available.

What a Kundli match can and cannot tell you

Astrology is guidance based on Vedic tradition, and no specific result is guaranteed. A Kundli match offers an astrological reading; it cannot make the decision for you or replace the couple’s own judgement and conversations.
